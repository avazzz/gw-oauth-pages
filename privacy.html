Privacy policy

Personal Workspace Connector is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts.

  • Gmail access is read-only. The tool does not send, change or delete messages.
  • Data is read only on the owner's request. To answer that request, the content is sent to the AI assistant service the owner uses (Anthropic Claude). It is not sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never published.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
